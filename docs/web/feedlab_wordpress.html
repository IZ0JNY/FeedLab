FeedLab is a free, open-source program to design feeds for microwave EME dishes, from 10 GHz up to 134 GHz.

FeedLab does not pretend to compete with anybody or with the excellent tools many hams already use. Its aim is simpler: thanks to a fast mode-matching engine, it lets you play with the parameters of the best-known feeds in the literature and see at once what happens to illumination, spillover, efficiency, G/T and S11 on your own dish — for our EME hobby the feed is a fundamental component. When you want to go deeper, openEMS is built in, so the same design can be checked with a full-wave simulation without leaving the program.

The other half of the idea is construction. FeedLab produces the 3D files (STEP and STL), dimensioned PDF drawings with tolerances, the λ/4 matching plate from the rectangular waveguide with the flange holes, the feed + plate assembly and a full project report. In this way anybody can have a feed made, even without any CAD knowledge, for example through the online CNC services (JLCCNC, PCBWay, Weerg: their materials and surface finishes are in the program).

What is inside

  • Feeds: open circular guide, W2IMU dual-mode, W2IMU Dual-Mode Feedhorn, parametric calculation OE2IGL, Large W2IMU flare, W1GHZ/W1FKF, Skobelev dual-mode, Pickett-Potter, conical horn, VE4MA/Super-VE4MA choke, Chaparral, Axial Corrugated Feedhorn CT1DMK, parametric calculation OE2IGL, rectangular horn, G3RPE and the Dual-Flare, IZ0JNY.
  • Dishes: catalogue of prime-focus and offset dishes with real data, or your own custom dish; edge taper of your choice; guided procedure that suggests the best feed.
  • Simulation: fast mode-matching simulator for patterns, efficiencies, phase centre, S11 and G/T; tolerance analysis; openEMS full-wave check of the same geometry.
  • Outputs: STEP/STL, PDF drawings, matching plate, assembly, project file (.json) and project reports in PDF and CSV.
  • Windows 10/11 installer (no administrator rights) or portable zip; English and Italian, with a detailed user manual.
